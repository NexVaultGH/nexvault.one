How Scores Are Calculated

1000 points total across four categories. Scores are computed live from on-chain data every time you check — nothing is stored, nothing can be manipulated.

Deposit Strength
250 pts

Based on total active USDX deposited across all your locked positions.

Any active deposit+30 pts
≥ $5,000 USDX active+50 pts
≥ $25,000 USDX active+80 pts
≥ $100,000 USDX active+90 pts

Cumulative. $100K gets all four bonuses = 250 pts.

Lock Commitment
250 pts

Points for the highest active lock tier. Not cumulative — only the best tier counts.

1-Year Lock active+20 pts
3-Year Lock active+80 pts
5-Year Lock active+250 pts

5-Year lock is the only path to Elite.

Protocol Loyalty
250 pts

Age of your oldest active deposit. Minimum 60 days before any points. 3 years to max.

≥ 60 days active20 pts
≥ 180 days active50 pts
≥ 365 days active100 pts
≥ 730 days active175 pts
≥ 1,095 days (3yr)250 pts

Not cumulative — the bracket your age falls into determines points.

Behavioral Excellence
250 pts

Five independent behaviors. Each earns independently — stack all five for maximum points.

Compounded yield 3+ times+30 pts
5+ lifetime deposits total+50 pts
3+ active referrals+60 pts
Genesis Badge + $5K+ active+60 pts
2+ simultaneous active deposits+50 pts

All five are independent. Max 250 if all conditions met.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about how NexCredit works.

When does my score update?
Your score is recalculated live every time you check it. There is no cached value — it reads directly from your current vault state on-chain.
Can I see another wallet's score?
No. The smart contract enforces this at the code level. Any call from a wallet that is not the score owner or the protocol OWNER will revert with a NotAuthorized error.
Why is Elite so hard to reach?
Elite requires $100K deposited, an active 5-Year lock, 3 years of continuous protocol loyalty, and behavioral consistency. All conditions must be met simultaneously. This makes it a genuine mark of extraordinary commitment — under 1% of wallets will qualify.
Does my score affect my yield?
Not at this time. NexCredit is informational — it reflects your protocol standing and may be used by future Nexus ecosystem protocols to gate access or offer preferential terms.
Where is the contract deployed?
NexCredit.sol will be deployed on Nexus mainnet at launch. The contract address will be published here and verifiable on the Nexus block explorer. Source code is open at github.com/NexVaultGH/nexvault-contracts.
