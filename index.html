WHAT IS NEXVAULT

A vault that pays you
every second

NexVault is a non-custodial USDX savings protocol on the Nexus blockchain. Deposit USDX stablecoins into an immutable smart contract, choose a lock period, and earn fixed yield automatically.

Yield is generated through GYDS (Global Yield Distribution System), which channels real U.S. Treasury yield on-chain. Every yield event is recorded, verifiable, and transparent.

Non-Custodial

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to sovereign yield

01
Connect Your Wallet
Install MetaMask on desktop or mobile. Connect to NexVault. Your wallet is your identity, your key, and your proof of ownership.
02
Deposit USDX & Choose Tier
Enter any amount of USDX. Select a 1-Year, 3-Year, or 5-Year lock period. Longer locks earn higher APY.
03
Earn Yield Automatically
Yield accrues every second, powered by GYDS. When your lock period ends, withdraw your principal plus all earned yield.
