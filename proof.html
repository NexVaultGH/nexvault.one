How this works
What is GYDS?
The Global Yield Distribution System is the protocol on Nexus that sources U.S. Treasury yield and delivers it to participating vaults. When GYDS sends yield to NexVault, the vault contract emits a GYDSYieldReceived event with the exact amount and timestamp.
What is a proof receipt?
Every GYDSYieldReceived event is a permanent record on the Nexus blockchain. It cannot be altered, deleted, or faked. Anyone can verify any receipt by looking up the transaction hash in any Nexus block explorer.
Why does this matter?
Traditional yield products ask you to trust a dashboard number. NexVault shows you every yield delivery as a cryptographic fact — proven by the Nexus zkVM, stamped into the chain, and publicly auditable by anyone with a browser.
What is the proof hash?
Each receipt includes a proof hash — the keccak256 digest of the transaction hash, vault address, and amount. It is a compact, deterministic fingerprint you can independently reproduce and verify against the on-chain data.
